# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Discuss technical choices, not personal attributes. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and disclosure of sensitive document contents are unacceptable. Use synthetic examples in public discussions.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content and restrict participation for violations. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository host; a dedicated confidential contact has not yet been designated. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid conflicts of interest. Do not post private reports publicly.
